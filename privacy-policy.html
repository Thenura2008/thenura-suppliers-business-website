Privacy Policy

Thenura Suppliers respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, phone, email, WhatsApp, social media, and other communication channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our hardware supply, construction material production, and construction-related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or use our website:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Address or project location
  • Details about your inquiry, quotation request, or project requirement
  • Images, documents, or project details voluntarily shared by you
  • Basic website usage information, such as browser type, pages visited, and general analytics data, if analytics tools are used

We do not collect online payments through this website, and we do not provide customer login accounts on this website.

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information through:

  • Contact forms
  • WhatsApp inquiry links
  • Phone calls
  • Email inquiries
  • Social media messages
  • Direct business communications
  • Website analytics tools, if enabled

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Prepare quotations
  • Provide information about our products and services
  • Arrange delivery or site visits
  • Communicate about construction, supply, or production-related services
  • Improve our website, customer service, and business operations
  • Maintain business records where required

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share necessary information only with trusted parties when required to provide our services, such as:

  • Delivery teams
  • Site inspection teams
  • Suppliers or subcontractors
  • Construction project teams
  • Professional advisors, where required
  • Government or legal authorities, if required by law

5. Data Protection and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no online communication method is completely secure, and users are encouraged to avoid sending highly sensitive information through general website forms or unsecured channels.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or analytics tools to understand website performance and visitor behavior. These tools help us improve the website and customer experience.

If advertising or tracking tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Customer Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal, business, and record-keeping requirements.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Google Maps, or other external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party platforms.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this website with the updated date.
